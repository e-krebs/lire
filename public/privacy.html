Lire privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026.

Lire is a personal feed reader. It has one user, its owner, who runs it for themselves. It shows articles from the owner's NewsBlur account. This page says what the app does with data.

What the app handles

  • The owner's NewsBlur account. It gives the feeds, the articles and the read state. Lire sends read, subscribe and unsubscribe actions back to NewsBlur.
  • The owner's email address. Cloudflare Access checks it when the owner signs in. Lire accepts only the owner's address.

Where the data goes

  • A Cloudflare Worker sits between the app and NewsBlur. It signs in to NewsBlur with the owner's credentials. They are stored as Worker secrets. The NewsBlur session cookie is kept in a Cloudflare Durable Object.
  • The app keeps a cache of feeds and articles in the device's browser storage.
  • Lire does not sell data, does not share it with other parties, and does not show ads. It has no analytics and no tracking.

Third parties

The data passes through two services. NewsBlur holds the feeds and the account. Cloudflare hosts the app and runs the sign-in. Their own privacy policies apply to the data they hold.

The public demo

The demo at demo.lire.krebs.tech shows sample feeds. It needs no account and collects nothing.

Deleting data

Clear the app's storage in the device settings to remove the local cache. To remove the account data, delete the NewsBlur account at NewsBlur.

Contact
